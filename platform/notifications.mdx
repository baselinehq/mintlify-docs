---

The Notifications page under Settings controls the emails CostGraph sends you. Toggle each on or off, then Save changes.

<img src="/images/platform/notifications.png" alt="Notification preferences" />

- Weekly cost summary: a Monday digest of your spend.
- Anomaly alerts: unexpected cost spikes and unusual changes.
- Savings recommendations: new cost-saving recommendations.
- GraphAI report ready: when a generated report is ready to review.
- Team activity: invites and role updates.
- Product updates: new features and release notes.
