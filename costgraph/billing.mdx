CostGraph offers flexible pricing tiers to meet your infrastructure monitoring and cost optimization needs. Subscriptions are required for the Pricing API, CostGraph operator, and VM agent.

For detailed pricing information and to select a plan, visit our [pricing page](https://costgraph.baselinehq.cloud/pricing).
