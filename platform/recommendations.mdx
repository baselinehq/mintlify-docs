CostGraph analyzes your usage and surfaces recommendations to cut spend. The total available shows as Potential cost impact on the [dashboard](/platform/cost-dashboard).

## Where recommendations appear

Recommendations are attached to the resources they apply to:

- Kubernetes: open a cluster and select the Workloads tab. Each workload shows its worst action, urgency, current cost, and monthly savings; the summary cards total the workloads with recommendations and the savings from applying them all.
- Virtual machines: the Virtual Machines list and each VM's detail page show a rightsizing recommendation per instance.

<img src="/images/platform/cluster-workloads.png" alt="Workload rightsizing recommendations with worst action, urgency, and monthly savings" />

## What gets recommended

- Rightsizing: match CPU and memory to real usage. For Kubernetes that means lowering requests and limits (*Downsize request*, *Downsize request and limit*); for VMs it means moving to a smaller or cheaper instance type.
- Idle cleanup: resources running consistently near zero, flagged for removal.

Each recommendation carries an urgency (low, medium, high) and an estimated monthly saving. Click a workload to see the per-container detail behind it.
