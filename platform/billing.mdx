title: Billing & plans
description: "Pay-as-you-go usage, invoices, and payment"
---

The Billing page under Settings shows your plan, current usage, and invoices. CostGraph is pay-as-you-go: usage is metered and billed at the end of each month.

<img src="/images/platform/billing.png" alt="Billing, pay-as-you-go usage for the period" />

Usage this period breaks the bill down by meter, connected Kubernetes clusters, virtual machines, metrics-only agents, and Pricing and Chat API requests, updated in real time. Use Manage billing to update your payment method and view invoices.
