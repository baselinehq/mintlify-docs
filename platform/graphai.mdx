GraphAI answers cost and Kubernetes questions in plain language and returns charts, tables, and analysis. Open it from the sidebar, or use the Ask GraphAI bar at the bottom of most pages.

<img src="/images/platform/graphai.png" alt="GraphAI, ask cost and Kubernetes questions in natural language" />

Type a question, "Which service had the highest cost this week?", "Where can I reduce costs based on current trends?", or pick one of the suggested prompts. Choose a persona to tune answers to your role.

Past conversations are saved under Recents, where you can search them or share one with a link.
