---
title: Autumn
description: "Report attributed cloud spend to an Autumn meter"
---

CostGraph reports each customer's attributed cloud spend to an
[Autumn](https://useautumn.com) meter as usage events. Autumn turns them into
invoices through Stripe. The shared model, tag rules, and nightly behavior are
on the [Billing exports](/costgraph/integrations/exports/overview) page.

## Before you begin

- Create a meter in Autumn and attach a price to it. Price per cent, or use
  Autumn's billing units to group 100 units into one priced dollar.
- Copy a secret key from **Developer > Secret keys**. A sandbox key exercises
  the whole flow without invoicing anyone.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the account">
    In **Integrations**, click **Autumn** and enter the secret key. CostGraph
    verifies the key against Autumn before it saves anything, and stores it
    encrypted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bind a meter">
    Bind an `autumn_` tag key to one of your Autumn meters through the
    CostGraph API.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tag spend by customer

The tag value must be an Autumn customer id. Autumn creates a customer the
first time it sees an id, so a mistyped tag value becomes a new billable
customer. Check the tag's values before you bind the meter, and review what
was reported after any rule change.

## Event payload

Each customer-day is one usage event on the meter, carrying the shared
reconciliation fields as properties you can group by in Autumn.

## Autumn behavior

Autumn bills every event into the customer's current cycle. A correction to an
old day appears on the next invoice, and `usage_date` shows which day it
belongs to.
