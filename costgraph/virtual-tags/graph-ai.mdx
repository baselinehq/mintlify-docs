Graph AI writes the rules behind a tag from a plain-English description, so you
can create a tag without building each rule by hand.

## Generating tags

- **Prompt while creating a tag.** Describe how the tag should be applied and
  choose **Generate rules**.
- **From the Suggestions tab.** Graph AI proposes whole tags across your spend,
  and you apply the ones you want.

### Give it context

Suggestions are only as good as what Graph AI knows about your organization.
Under **Add context to your tag suggestions**, name the teams, environments, or
products you split spend by. That produces far better suggestions than describing
the cloud.
