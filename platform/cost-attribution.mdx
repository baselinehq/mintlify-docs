title: Cost attribution
description: "How CostGraph maps spend to clusters, namespaces, workloads, and VMs"
---

CostGraph breaks every cost into the resources behind it, CPU, memory, and storage, on the dashboard (by provider), on the Compute lists, and on every resource detail page.

<img src="/images/platform/clusters.png" alt="Kubernetes clusters, cost broken into CPU, memory, and storage" />

## Kubernetes

Open a cluster from the Kubernetes Clusters page under Compute to see its cost by:

- Namespace and node (the Nodes tab).
- Workload: cost and savings per workload on the Workloads tab, grouped by controller.
- Container: open a workload to see its containers.

<img src="/images/platform/cluster-workloads.png" alt="Per-workload cost inside a cluster" />

## Virtual machines

A VM's cost is its System cost, split into CPU, memory, and storage. Open a VM to see its containers and processes ranked by CPU and memory usage.

<img src="/images/platform/vm-overview.png" alt="Virtual machine detail, system cost and what's driving the load" />

## Efficiency

Every resource also shows its efficiency, how much of what you provisioned is actually used.
