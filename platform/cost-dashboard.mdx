The dashboard is your home in CostGraph: a single view of spend, efficiency, and what needs attention across every connected cluster and cloud account.

<img src="/images/platform/dashboard.png" alt="CostGraph dashboard, infrastructure overview" />

## Infrastructure overview

The cards at the top summarize the selected period, set it with the date selector at the top right, and use Export to download the underlying data.

- Total spend: your cost across all providers for the period.
- Potential cost impact: the monthly savings available across your open [recommendations](/platform/recommendations).
- Active anomalies: unusual cost or usage changes CostGraph has detected.
- Cluster efficiency: how much of your provisioned cluster capacity is actually used.
- Cost by provider: spend split across your clouds, with a per-provider breakdown. See [Cost attribution](/platform/cost-attribution) for how spend is mapped down to clusters, workloads, and VMs.

## Needs attention

Below the overview, the Needs attention table surfaces the issues ranked by impact, severity, the affected resource, cost impact, and how long each has been active. Search or filter to narrow the list, and select a row to investigate.

## Ask GraphAI

The bar at the bottom lets you ask cost and Kubernetes questions in natural language, "Why did spend spike this month?", "Show idle compute resources", without leaving the page.
