---
title: How CostGraph works
---

CostGraph turns the data your clusters and VMs already produce into cost insight and rightsizing recommendations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect">
    Install the [operator](/costgraph/operator/overview) on a Kubernetes cluster or the [agent](/costgraph/agent) on a VM. Each collects resource usage and inventory and remote-writes it to CostGraph. Only metrics leave your environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attribute">
    CostGraph maps spend to the resources behind it. For Kubernetes, down to namespace, workload, and container; for VMs, at the instance, split into CPU, memory, and storage. Prices come from the [Pricing API](/api-reference/introduction), covering all major clouds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recommend">
    From observed CPU and memory usage over a rolling window, CostGraph recommends rightsizing (lowering Kubernetes requests and limits, or moving a VM to a smaller instance) and flags idle resources.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act">
    Review the savings in the [platform](/platform/cost-dashboard), ask [GraphAI](/platform/graphai) in plain language, or pull it all programmatically over [MCP](/costgraph/mcp/install).
  </Step>
</Steps>
