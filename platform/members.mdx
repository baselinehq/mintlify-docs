---
title: Members & workspaces
description: "Invite members, assign roles, and manage workspace access"
---

CostGraph organizes access at two levels, both under Settings:

- An organization holds your members and billing.
- Each organization has one or more workspaces: isolated environments with their own data, members, and settings. Switch workspaces from the selector at the top left.

## Members and roles

The Members page under Organization Settings lists everyone in the organization with their role: Owner, Admin, or Member. Use Invite member to add someone by email; pending invitations appear below, and you can deactivate users.

<img src="/images/platform/members.png" alt="Organization members with roles and pending invites" />

## Workspace access

The Members page under Tenants Settings controls which organization users can access the current workspace. Each workspace also keeps its own customisation and muted-anomaly settings, which apply only to that workspace.
