The Kubernetes Clusters page under Compute lists every connected cluster with its cost, node count, change, and potential savings.

<img src="/images/platform/clusters.png" alt="Kubernetes Clusters list with cost and savings per cluster" />

Open a cluster for its detail tabs:

- Overview: cost, efficiency, node and pod counts, and CPU and memory usage over time.
- Nodes: each node's cost and utilization; open a node for its containers and metrics.
- Workloads: cost, savings, and a [rightsizing recommendation](/platform/recommendations) per workload, grouped by controller; open a workload for its containers.
- Persistent volumes: capacity, usage, and cost per volume.

<img src="/images/platform/cluster-overview.png" alt="Cluster detail, overview with cost, efficiency, and usage charts" />

## Metrics

CPU, memory, network, disk, and persistent-volume utilization over time, for the cluster, its nodes, workloads, and containers. Pick a time range, or add another cluster to compare.

<img src="/images/platform/cluster-metrics.png" alt="Cluster metrics, CPU, memory, network, and disk over time" />

## Recommendations

On the Workloads tab, select a workload to open its recommendation: the CPU and memory changes (current request and limit vs recommended) and the savings each unlocks.

<img src="/images/platform/workload-recommendation.png" alt="Workload recommendation, current vs recommended CPU and memory, with savings" />
